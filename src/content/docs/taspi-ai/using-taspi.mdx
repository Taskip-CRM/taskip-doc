## Opening the panel

Click Taspi's brain icon (top bar, or inline on a record) to slide open the chat panel. Every
fresh, unscoped conversation greets you by name with a short status line, for example how many
invoices, tasks, or leads you have, and four quick-action cards you can click instead of typing.
Opened from a specific record instead, the greeting and quick actions are about that record, for
example opening it from a lead offers *"Suggest a follow-up"*, *"Add a note"*, or *"Convert to
contact"*.

## A conversation, step by step

<Steps>

1. **Ask for something**

   Type in plain language and press Enter (Shift+Enter for a new line), or click a quick-action
   card. For example:
   - *"Create invoice for Acme Inc."*
   - *"Show unpaid invoices"*
   - *"Move the Acme deal to the next stage"*
   - *"Show open tickets"*

2. **Watch it think**

   While Taspi works, a live list of steps builds up above its reply (for example looking up a
   client, then drafting the invoice), each one checked off as it completes. Once the reply is
   ready, this collapses to a single line like *"3 steps taken"* that you can expand any time to
   see what it actually did.

3. **Confirm anything that changes data**

   Creating, sending, or updating something always stops for your confirmation first, nothing is
   created or sent silently. You'll see a card headed **"Ready to [action]"** with a one-line
   summary of what's about to happen, and **Confirm** / **Cancel** buttons. Sending or replying to
   an email gets a fuller preview card instead, with the actual subject and body so you can read
   before it goes out. Actions Taskip considers destructive (like deleting something) are shown
   with a red accent instead of the default blue, so they stand out. If one request needs several
   confirmable steps, each one is labeled *"#2 this request"*, *"#3 this request"*, and so on, so
   you can tell how many are left.

4. **Get a typed result card**

   Once confirmed (or for read-only requests, immediately), Taspi replies with a card shaped for
   what you asked about, an invoice card shows the invoice's number, client, amount, and line
   items; a dashboard-stats card shows revenue, contacts, and leads up top with paid/unpaid,
   completed/pending, and open/closed breakdowns underneath. Leads, contacts, companies, projects,
   tasks, and quotations each get their own card in the same style. Anything without a dedicated
   card layout still gets a readable generic result card, nothing is dropped silently.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  If a confirm attempt fails or times out, the same card stays on screen with an inline error and
  the button changes to **Try again**, you don't lose the request and have to start over.
</Aside>

## Conversation history

Click the history icon in the panel header to see your past conversations, each with a preview of
the last message, when it was updated, and how many messages it has. Click one to reopen it where
you left off, or click **New chat** to start fresh. The refresh icon in the header also starts a
new conversation without leaving the history view.

## Credits

The panel header shows how many AI credits you've used this billing period (or an infinity icon if
your plan is unlimited). Credits refresh after every response so the count is always current.
