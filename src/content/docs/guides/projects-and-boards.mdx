# Projects & Boards

## Creating a Project

From your workspace dashboard, click **+ New Project**. You can start from a template or a blank project.

### Project Templates

| Template | Best for |
|---|---|
| **Blank** | Custom workflows from scratch |
| **Kanban** | Ongoing work without fixed deadlines |
| **Sprint** | Agile development with 2-week cycles |
| **Bug Tracker** | Software issue tracking |
| **Marketing Calendar** | Campaign planning and content scheduling |

## Customizing Your Board

The Board view shows your tasks as cards in columns. Each column represents a **Status**.

### Adding & Renaming Columns

Click **+ Add Status** at the right of the board to add a new column. Double-click a column header to rename it.

### Reordering Columns

Drag any column header left or right to change the order.

### Color-Coding Columns

Click the ⋯ menu on a column → **Set color** to give it a background color. Useful for marking "Done" as green and "Blocked" as red.

## Filters & Grouping

Use the **Filter** button (top right of any view) to narrow down tasks:

- Filter by assignee, label, priority, due date, or custom field
- Combine multiple filters with AND / OR logic
- Save a filter as a **Saved View** for quick access

<Aside type="note">
  Saved views appear in the project's sidebar under **Views**. They're private to you by default, but you can share them with the team.
</Aside>

## Project Settings

Go to **Project → Settings** to configure:

- **Name & Description** — Shown on the dashboard and in search
- **Access** — Public (all workspace members) or Private (invite only)
- **Default View** — Which view opens when you enter the project
- **Custom Fields** — Add fields like "Story Points", "Department", or "External Link"
- **Automations** — Trigger actions based on conditions (e.g. "When status = Done, notify Slack")
