# Quick Start

Get your first project running in Taskip in just a few steps.

<Steps>

1. **Create your workspace**

   Go to [taskip.net](https://taskip.net) and sign up. During onboarding you'll create a **Workspace** — this is your organization's home in Taskip.

2. **Create a project**

   Click **+ New Project** from the dashboard. Give it a name and choose a template (Blank, Kanban, or Sprint).

3. **Add your first task**

   Inside your project, press `T` (or click **+ Add Task**). Fill in the title, set a due date, and assign it to yourself or a teammate.

4. **Invite your team**

   Go to **Settings → Members** and enter your teammates' email addresses. They'll receive an invite link to join your workspace.

5. **You're ready!**

   Explore the sidebar to switch between Board view, List view, and Timeline. Use `Cmd+K` (Mac) or `Ctrl+K` (Windows) to open the command palette.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note">
  On a free plan, you can have up to 5 members and 3 projects. [Upgrade to Pro](/getting-started/installation/) for unlimited access.
</Aside>

## Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|---|---|
| `T` | Create new task |
| `Cmd/Ctrl + K` | Open command palette |
| `G then P` | Go to Projects |
| `G then D` | Go to Dashboard |
| `?` | Show all shortcuts |
