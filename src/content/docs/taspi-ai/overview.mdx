Taspi is Taskip's **built-in AI agent**, already inside your workspace. Click its brain icon in
the top bar and a chat panel opens, right there in the app, no external tool or setup required.
Ask it to do something in plain language and it drafts invoices, creates tasks, moves deals,
replies to tickets, and more, checking with you first before anything gets created or sent.

<Aside type="tip">
  Taspi and the [MCP server](/mcp-server/overview/) are two different ways to bring AI into
  Taskip. Taspi is built in and lives inside your workspace UI, no connection to set up. The MCP
  server is for connecting *external* AI tools you already use, like Claude Code, Claude Desktop,
  Cursor, or ChatGPT, to your workspace. You can use either, or both, side by side.
</Aside>

## Who can use it

Taspi's icon only appears for people who actually have access to it. It's included for
**Administrators, Managers, and Members** on a paid, internal, or active trial plan with the AI
module enabled, **Clients never see it**. If your plan or role doesn't include Taspi, the icon is
simply not shown, there's no locked-out button or upgrade prompt in its place.

## Where you'll find it

- **Top bar** — a brain icon next to your notifications opens the main panel, available from any
  page in the workspace.
- **Inline on records** — the same brain icon appears on invoice, quotation, lead, contact, and
  company pages (including their Quick View sheets), and inside the email compose/reply view.
  Opening Taspi from one of these scopes the conversation to that specific record, for example
  opening it from an invoice greets you with options to check its status, send it, or update it,
  instead of a generic greeting.
